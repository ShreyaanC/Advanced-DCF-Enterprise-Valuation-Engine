How to use this Calculator

1. Setup Assumptions: Set your WACC, projection years, and terminal growth rate.

2. Input Cash Flows: Enter cash flows in the table. Year 0 is usually the initial investment.

3. Quick Paste: Paste a comma-separated list of numbers to auto-fill.

Metrics Explained

  • NPV: Net Present Value.
  • IRR: Internal Rate of Return.
  • Enterprise Value (EV): NPV of flows + PV of Terminal Value.
